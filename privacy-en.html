Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

Operator and contact

Voice-Memo is operated by nepro. Contact wideep98@naver.com about privacy or deletion requests. This policy applies to the current iPhone app (iOS 26 or later).

Records on your device

Notes, recordings, transcripts, and translations are stored on your device. The current iPhone app does not send recordings or transcripts to the operator’s server. Sharing and export send data to the destination you choose. Device backups depend on your settings.

On-device transcription and translation

Recordings are transcribed and text is translated on your device. Recordings and translation source text are not sent to the operator’s server or an external translation provider. Internet access and Apple services may be needed to download language data when you first use these features.

Pro purchases and Apple services

Pro purchases, restoration, and code redemption are handled through the Apple App Store. The app checks purchase results and entitlement status without sending purchase receipts to the operator’s server. Apple’s account, payment, and language download services are subject to Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Voice notes in Trash can be restored for 30 days. Expiry or permanent deletion removes recordings, transcripts, and translations from your device. The current iPhone app does not store notes, recordings, transcripts, or translations on the operator’s server. Email the operator to request deletion of server authentication information or server content from earlier versions.

Advertising and tracking

The app has no advertising or advertising tracking. Recordings, transcripts, and purchase information are not sold or used for advertising targeting.
